## Changes from the Apache ICLA V2.2

- "the Foundation" becomes "the Maintainer", defined in section 1 as the
  copyright holder of Oxyn and any successor under section 9;
- the Foundation's commitment to use Contributions consistently with its
  nonprofit status is removed: the Maintainer is not a nonprofit foundation;
- section 2 states explicitly that the Maintainer may relicense Contributions,
  which the Apache text allows through the right to sublicense;
- section 4 no longer refers to a Corporate CLA, which Oxyn does not have;
- section 9, "Assignment", is new;
- the form fields (name, address, e-mail, signature) are replaced by the
  signing process in CONTRIBUTING.md.
